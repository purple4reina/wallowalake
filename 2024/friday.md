## Music from Zambia: the Gule Wamkulu

We end the week somewhere very different from the concert hall.

Rey volunteered in Zambia with the **Chewa people** — subsistence farmers in rural villages with no electricity, dirt floors, and very limited access to education. Music is inseparable from community life.

**The Nyau** is a secret society of male dancers. **Gule Wamkulu** ("the great dance") is their ritual performance — held at funerals, initiations, and celebrations. Dancers wear masks representing animals and spirits, each with its own song and character. The ceremony brings the spirit world into the village.

**What to listen and watch for:**
- Who dances, who drums, who sings — and what is the role of the audience?
- Are the performers separate from the community, or part of it?
- How does the music work without written notation or sheet music?
- What do the masks and costumes communicate that words cannot?
