Before we can talk about music history, we need a shared vocabulary.
Today we build the toolkit you'll use all week to describe what you hear.

## Key Ideas

- We use **terminology** to describe sound — not feelings. "It's loud" not "it's exciting."
- **Pitch**: diatonic (in a key) vs. no pitch center (atonal)
- **Rhythm**: free and flowing vs. steady pulse vs. complex/layered
- **Texture**: how many independent musical lines?
  - **Monophony** — one melody, nothing else
  - **Homophony** — one melody with chordal support
  - **Polyphony** — multiple independent melodies at once
- **Form**: one idea throughout, or multiple contrasting ideas?
- By the end of this week you'll be able to identify a time period just by listening.
