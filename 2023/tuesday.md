Now that we have vocabulary, let's use it to map out the history of music —
and take a deep dive into the Baroque period.

## Key Ideas

- Music history has five main **periods**: Medieval/Renaissance → Baroque → Classical → Romantic → Modern
- Each period has characteristic **rhythm, texture, dynamics, pitch, and form**.
- **Baroque** (roughly 1600–1750): steady motor rhythms, mostly constant dynamics (no crescendos!), ornate melodies.
- Two key Baroque genres:
  - **Sonata** — chamber work for 1–4 players. Movements go slow–fast–slow–fast. A "Trio Sonata" has 3 lines but often uses 4 players (two melody instruments + harpsichord + cello for the bass).
  - **Concerto** — contrast between a large group (**ripieno**) and a small group (**concertino**). A **Concerto Grosso** uses a small group of soloists; a **Solo Concerto** features one.
