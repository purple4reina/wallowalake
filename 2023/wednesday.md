Two periods, one big shift: Classical music is elegant and balanced;
Romantic music pushes everything further — bigger emotions, bigger orchestras, bigger dynamic range.

## Key Ideas

- **Classical** (roughly 1750–1820): clear melodies, balanced phrases, homophonic texture, steady tempo, invention of the crescendo/diminuendo.
- **Romantic** (roughly 1820–1900): chromatic harmony, dramatic dynamic contrasts, irregular rhythms, larger orchestras.
- **Sonata-Allegro form** — the most important structure of the Classical/Romantic period:
  - **Exposition**: Theme I (home key) → Theme II (new key)
  - **Development**: themes get pulled apart and transformed
  - **Recapitulation**: both themes return in the home key
- **Minuet** — light, in 3, originally a dance. **Scherzo** ("joke") — same form, but after Beethoven: much faster, felt in 1.
- **Rondo** form: ABACABA — the opening theme keeps coming back between contrasting sections.
- Classical **symphony**: 4 movements — Sonata-Allegro, slow, minuet/scherzo, rondo.
- Classical **concerto**: 3 movements (no minuet) — the soloist enters on the repeat of the exposition.

      Sibelius — Symphony No. 1, III. Scherzo: Allegro
    </a>
    <span class="rec-note">Same ABA form as the minuet, but much faster and more energetic — this is what Beethoven unleashed.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HNUStPPKXN8" target="_blank" rel="noopener">

    </a>
    <span class="rec-note">Three movements: Sonata-Allegro, slow, rondo. Listen for where the soloist enters during the exposition.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3I9LlIwKqYk" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
      Stamitz — Sinfonia à 8 in E-flat Major
    </a>
    <span class="rec-note">Early Classical symphony from the Mannheim school — notice the dramatic crescendos, a brand new effect at the time.</span>

    </a>
    <span class="rec-note">Late Romantic: listen for the second theme entering around 5:00 — a complete change of character.</span>
  </li>
</ul>
