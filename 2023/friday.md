## Music from Zambia

We finish the week with something outside the Western classical tradition entirely.

Rey volunteered in Zambia with the **Chewa people** — subsistence farmers living in rural villages, with very little access to electricity or running water. Music is woven into daily life: in churches, community gatherings, and at home.

**What to listen and watch for in each video:**

- **Hildah's church** — How many independent parts? Who leads? Describe the texture and form.
- **Rural church** — How does illiteracy affect how music is learned and transmitted? Notice the role of the feet.
- **Community center** — What is the role of the audience? Are they separate from the performers?
- **Catholic church** — Describe the instruments: intonation, construction, tone quality. What is the meter? Watch Emmanuel carefully.
- **Kalimba** — A small plucked instrument held in both hands. Notice how syllabic the language is, and how the hands shape the sound.
