Welcome to music history! This week you'll build vocabulary for describing what you hear,
learn to identify musical periods, and make educated guesses about any piece of classical music.

## Key Ideas

- We use **terminology** to describe sound — not feelings. "It's loud" not "it's exciting."
- Every sound can be described using categories: **timbre, dynamics, articulation, pitch, rhythm, texture, form**.
- **Texture** describes how many independent musical lines there are:
  - **Monophony** — one melody, no accompaniment
  - **Homophony** — one melody with chordal support
  - **Polyphony** — multiple independent melodies at once
- **Dynamics** = how loud or soft, and whether that changes.
- Being a great listener means staying curious, making guesses, and being willing to be wrong.
