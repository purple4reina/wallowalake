From palace ballrooms to concert halls — how a dance form became one of the most
important structures in classical music.

## Key Ideas

- A Baroque **suite** is a collection of dances written for listening, not dancing.
- The **Minuet** was the most fashionable dance in 1700s France — it has a strict **ABA form**:
  Minuet (A) → Trio (B) → Minuet again (A, not repeated).
- The **Trio** is lighter and quieter, often using fewer instruments.
- Beethoven replaced the stately Minuet with the **Scherzo** ("joke") — same ABA form, same 3-beat meter, but *much* faster and more energetic.
- Question to think about: *How does a composer know when a piece is done?*
