What do "timbre," "polyphony," and "Romantic period" actually mean?
Today we build the vocabulary you'll use all week to describe what you hear.

## Key Ideas

- We use **terminology** to describe *sound* — not feelings. ("It's loud" not "it's exciting.")
- Every instrument belongs to a family: strings, winds, percussion, voice, or electronic.
- Music history has five main **periods**: Medieval/Renaissance → Baroque → Classical → Romantic → Modern
- **Polyphony** = multiple independent melodies happening at the same time (like a round, but fancier)
- A great listener stays curious, makes guesses, and asks questions.
