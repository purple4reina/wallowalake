In the 1800s, composers started collecting the folk songs of their homelands
and weaving them into concert music.

## Key Ideas

- **Folk songs** are traditional songs passed down through communities — often with no known composer.
- **Nationalism** in the 19th century inspired composers to celebrate their country's culture, identity, and history in music.
- **Bartók** traveled rural Hungary, recording folk songs by hand and on early phonographs; **Kodály** used folk music as the foundation for Hungarian music education.
- You'll recognize some of the composers from this week's camp repertoire: Holst, Grainger, and Copland all appear today.
