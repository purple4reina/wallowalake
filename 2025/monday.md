Before we can talk about music history, we need a shared vocabulary.
Today we practice using musical terms to describe *exactly* what we hear — not how it makes us feel.

## Key Ideas

- Terminology describes **sound**, not emotion. "Fast tempo" not "exciting." "High register" not "bright."
- Key dimensions of any piece: **tempo, instrumentation, rhythm, register, texture, scales/pitch center, form**
- **Texture**: how many independent lines?
  - **Monophony** — one melody alone
  - **Homophony** — melody + chordal support
  - **Polyphony** — multiple independent melodies at once
- Western scales are **diatonic** (in a key). Other music uses **quarter-tones**, **pentatonic**, **modal**, or **no pitch center** at all.
- By the end of the week you'll hear a piece of classical music for the first time and make an educated guess about when it was written.
